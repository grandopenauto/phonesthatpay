What does an AI Test Pilot do?

An AI Test Pilot is our name for a participant who could help verify client-authorized digital work using a phone, following disclosed instructions, consent requirements and human review. We start with compatible Android devices and real-device QA. When a paying project exists, eligible participants may be invited to complete an explicitly described assignment.

This program is being designed within HDP's SI8 Business Operating Systems Architect & Operator approach.

Start with what you have

You do not need to purchase a phone to express interest, and any future optional device purchase will be separate from work eligibility.

Own the decision

No hidden scraping, unsolicited messages, cross-app control, or always-on remote access. Tasks require clear permission and participant choice.

Know the terms before work

Available work, any device requirements, performance criteria, payment timing, and potential expenses will be disclosed before acceptance. No earnings are guaranteed.

The ground rules

A program built on trust, not hype.

We intend to use a free-to-express-interest model. Applications are not employment offers, assignments are not guaranteed, and participant payments are contingent on approved, client-funded work under a separate agreement.

No one should spend money on a phone expecting income from PhonesThatPay. If specialized devices become useful, compatibility and optional purchasing choices will be transparent, with no pay-to-access-work arrangement.

Some tasks or locations may require identity verification, eligibility checks, tax documentation, or additional consent. We will review applicable employment and consumer rules before paid onboarding.
