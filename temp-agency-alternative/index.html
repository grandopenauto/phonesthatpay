Honest expectations

A pilot isn't a job offer.

Submitting your interest does not create an employment relationship or a paid contract. We will not require you to purchase hardware in exchange for potential work. Actual opportunities, classification, compensation and device permissions require separate arrangements.

The SI8 name refers to HDP's operating-systems architecture and operations approach—not a government-issued employment credential or a staffing license.

Frequently asked questions

Is an AI workforce a temp agency alternative?

Is PhonesThatPay a temp agency?

No. PhonesThatPay is currently an HDP pilot venture for potential human-supervised mobile work and managed digital outcomes. It does not currently place workers into shifts, maintain an open job board, or provide temp staffing.

Can I hire a digital worker through HDP today?

You can submit a scoped business inquiry for review. A paid service would require verification of feasibility, devices, consent, operating requirements, price, and a written agreement. There is not yet an on-demand fleet of available digital workers.

What makes a pilot different from a staffing shift?

A scoped pilot focuses on an acceptance-tested result rather than filling a general job role. Suitable digital tasks may be partially automated, but human oversight and approved device actions are still important.

Can I apply for temp agency work here?

No active jobs or placements are available through this site. If you want to learn about a potential future participant program, read the AI Test Pilot beta page, and use conventional employment resources for immediate job opportunities.

How are participants classified and compensated?

Those details have not been established for paid operations. HDP must assess applicable labor and worker-classification rules, including California law, and disclose terms before any paid assignments. Expressing interest is free.
