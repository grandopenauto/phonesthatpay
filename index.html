Our pilot principles ↗
Straight answers

Good questions.
Clear expectations.

Can I start earning money today?

Not through this website. PhonesThatPay is in the founding pilot stage. We are developing business demand, participant onboarding, verification, and payment procedures. Any opportunities will depend on actual paid customer assignments.

Do I have to buy a special phone?

No. Expressing interest is free and does not require buying a phone. Some future assignments may have technical device requirements, but buying hardware will never guarantee an assignment or earnings.

Will HDP control my phone?

Not by joining this website. Any future device actions must be within disclosed, approved task scope and compatible with Android rules. Our existing proof-of-concept app does not enable general remote control.

How much can a participant make?

There is no supported earnings estimate yet. Rates, assignment availability, review standards, and payment terms must be established and disclosed before any paid work begins.

What if my business needs a whole AI team?

HDP can discuss a separate managed-cloud workflow, including research, QA, process operations, and human review. Scope, security controls, deliverables, and pricing are agreed before activation.

Does this website collect my information?

The initial static website has no built-in tracking or submission database. The interest form opens a draft in your own email application; nothing is sent until you send the email. See our privacy page.
