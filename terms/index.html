Know the stage.

PhonesThatPay is currently exploring a founding participant program and initial business projects. No live work marketplace has launched through this site.

Information dated October 12, 2026. These are informational pilot conditions, not a substitute for a future paid-work agreement, complete platform terms, employment documentation, or client contract.

No job or income guarantee

Submitting a beta interest email does not create an employment, contractor, business opportunity, or paid assignment relationship. There is no guaranteed access to tasks, fixed work volume, passive earning stream, or income level. Payment terms, when available, must be presented separately before accepting paid work.

No required purchase

You may inquire about the program for free. No hardware purchase or fee is required to submit interest, and any optional supported device sale will not guarantee work or compensation.

Consent and appropriate use

Future tasks, where offered, must be lawful, client-authorized, and within the participant's express permission. This initiative does not authorize circumventing app security, covert surveillance, unauthorized message sending, scraping restricted data, or evading Android platform rules.

Business engagement

Businesses expressing interest are requesting a discussion—not placing an order. Separate written scope, pricing, acceptance criteria, data protection, and payment terms are required before paid fulfillment.

Program changes

HDP may revise pilot scope, eligibility, and availability as technology, demand, and legal requirements are evaluated. Material operating terms will be disclosed before any live paid assignments are introduced.

For pilot terms or participant concerns, email contact@highestdegreepriorities.com.
